# Advanced Geofall assets

Flags: hampusborgos/country-flags, derived from Wikimedia Commons national-flag SVGs. Existing20 flags at their original URLs also corrected from icon4:3 variants to national proportions. Original aspect ratios and symbol geometry retained; root rasterization size set to1000px for local canvas decoding. Author identifies national flags as public domain in https://github.com/hampusborgos/country-flags/blob/main/README.md . SourceURL and SHA256 perfile: docs/agent-work/geofall-2/flag-audit.json.

Country outlines: Natural Earth50m Admin0 dataset, public domain (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). Largest mainland polygon, local equirectangular scaling; didactic silhouettes rather than official boundary maps.

Monument illustrations: original schematic SVGs authored for this project, from verified UNESCO/site descriptions. No photos, captions, title or answer text inside graphics. Source locations in each new question.

Country metadata: GeoNames, CC BY attribution, https://www.geonames.org/export/ . Build-time snapshot in docs/agent-work/geofall-2/geonames.txt.
